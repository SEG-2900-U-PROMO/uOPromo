Features

Discover personalized deals, tailored to your liking.

uOPromo was built for students to discover personalized deals, tailored to their liking. The application offers a personal customization feature and filtering bar, to enhance user experience making finding deals one button away from finding your dream deal. Explore a variety so you don’t have FOMO.

Found a must not miss deal, and want to share?

Don’t fear! uOPromo has a feature just for you if you found a must not miss deal, and want to share?. Share deals easily and be an active contributor to the community.

Top-tier Coupon verification service

uOPromo uses a two-level verification system to ensure users do not receive scams, ensuring coupons validity.

Direct Business Verification: We partner with businesses directly to verify the validity of coupons, ensuring users do not face scams.

User verification: Users using the coupon may also complete an optional form to verify the validity of the coupon offer.
